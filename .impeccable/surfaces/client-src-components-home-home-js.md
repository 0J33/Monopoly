# Home, lobby & game menus

Scope: home screen (create / join), lobby, and every in-game menu (title deed, my deeds, auction, trade, card draw, victory, chat and trades drawers). Visitor mode: Operate. The board itself is the settled world and is not redesigned.

Audience/job: friends, mostly on desktop over a call, getting into a room fast and then managing deeds, trades and auctions mid-game without losing the table.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every menu is a printed game component dealt onto the felt — decks, title deeds, auction and rule cards — refusing the dark-dashboard card grid of the old screens.
OWN-WORLD: Board felt green and gold hairlines as the table; cream card stock with ink type and printed frame lines; Chance orange, Chest blue and property colour bands; Barlow Condensed caps for printed headings, Inter body, mono money.
STORY: A player sees two decks — start or join — puts their name on their place card, and is at a table in seconds; in play, every property is a deed card they can hold.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Felt table; wordmark top-left; two card stacks centred side by side — orange "Start a game" (board choice + deal), blue "Join a game" (code + open tables); place card with name and tokens beneath both.
FORM: Chance & Chest decks, position 6 of 7, seed abc8b385. Raised from The Rulebook: printed-sheet type discipline — heavy condensed headings, ruled rows, real card-stock depth.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
